FAQ

Common questions

Everything you need to know about working with us.

How quickly can you start?
Typically within 1–2 weeks of our first call. We take on a limited number of concurrent projects so each gets focused attention. If you have an urgent timeline, mention it during the call.
Do you work with existing codebases?
Yes. About half our engagements involve integrating agents or automation into existing systems. We'll review your codebase during discovery and propose an approach that fits your current architecture — no forced rewrites.
What AI models do you support?
We are model-agnostic: OpenAI, Anthropic, open-source models hosted on your infrastructure, or hybrid setups. The choice depends on your latency, cost, privacy requirements, and whether you need specialized fine-tuning.
Who owns the code and IP?
You retain 100% ownership of all source code, agent configurations, and documentation upon completion. We retain no ownership. You can take everything in-house at any point.
Do you offer ongoing maintenance?
Every project includes post-launch support. Studio and Enterprise plans include continuous maintenance, monitoring, and updates as your needs evolve.
What industries do you work with?
We work across fintech, healthcare, legal, SaaS, e-commerce, and more. Our experience spans startups to enterprise organizations looking to automate complex workflows.
