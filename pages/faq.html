Common questions

Everything you need to know about working with us.

How quickly can you start? +

Typically within 1–2 weeks after our first call. We take on a limited number of concurrent projects so each gets focused attention.

Do you work with existing codebases? +

Yes. About half our engagements involve integrating agents or automation into existing systems without forced rewrites.

What AI models do you support? +

We're model-agnostic: OpenAI, Anthropic, open-source models hosted on your infrastructure, or hybrid setups.

Who owns the code and IP? +

You retain 100% ownership of all source code, agent configurations, and documentation upon completion.

Do you offer ongoing maintenance? +

Every project includes post-launch support. Studio and Enterprise plans include continuous monitoring and updates.
